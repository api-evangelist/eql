# EQL

> EQL is the infrastructure behind fair launches for high-demand, limited-supply products — sneakers, collectibles, art, coins, alcohol, trading cards, and brand collaborations. Every EQL launch is certified Run Fair®, using bot mitigation, resilient infrastructure, and fair allocation to put products in the hands of genuine fans, not bots and resellers.

EQL serves two audiences: **brands and retailers** (who run launches) and **fans** (who enter for a shot at the products they want). The platform solves the problems that plague high-heat drops — bots, fraud, site crashes, unreliable checkout — while giving brands real-time data, seamless payment capture, allocation control, and secondary sales opportunities. It works for everyone from boutique retailers to enterprise brands, and includes a dedicated Shopify App. Brands running launches on EQL include Nike, Crocs, Funko, Foot Locker, Undefeated, Stanley, and Sullivans Cove. EQL is based in Melbourne, Australia, on the land of the Wurundjeri people of the Kulin Nation.
